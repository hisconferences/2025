HIS 2025 @ Bandung

Authors' Instructions

General Instructions

  • Presentation Language: The presentation language of the HIS 2025 is English.
  • Presentation Time: The total time for each regular paper is 30 minutes.
  • Attendance Confirmation: You should confirm your attendance with the Session Chair at least 5 minutes before the session.
Important Note

All presentation times are in West Indonesia Timezone (+7 GMT). Please adjust your schedule accordingly.

Replacement Policy

The co-author may be replaced if notified 24 hours in advance. Please send an email to his2025@ideas-lab.org with the replacement presenter's information.

Presentation File Preparation

1.1 Slide Format

  • File format: PPTX or PDF.
  • Aspect ratio: 16:9 (widescreen).
  • Max slides: 15–18 slides.
  • Mandatory slides: Title, Introduction, Method, Results, Conclusion, Acknowledgment.
  • Fonts: Use standard fonts (Calibri, Arial, Times New Roman).

1.2 File Naming

Please name your presentation file as follows:

PaperID_PresenterName.pptx

Example: HIS2025_001_JohnDoe.pptx

1.3 Submission Deadline

Upload Presentation File

Important

Please test your presentation on a different computer before uploading to ensure compatibility.

Onsite Presenter Requirements

2.1 Arrival & Registration

  • Please arrive at the conference venue at least 1 hour before your session.
  • Register at the registration desk upon arrival.
  • Confirm your attendance with the Session Chair at least 5 minutes before the session.

2.2 Equipment Provided

  • Projector
  • Laser pointer
  • HDMI cable
  • Laptop
  • Microphone

2.3 Dress Code

Business formal or smart casual attire is recommended for all presenters.

Tips for Onsite Presenters
  • Bring a backup copy of your presentation on a USB drive.
  • Practice your presentation timing to stay within the allocated 30 minutes.
  • Arrive early to familiarize yourself with the presentation room.

Instructions for Session Chairs

Session Chair Responsibilities

  • You should join the room at least 10 minutes before the session.
  • You can determine the exact presentation time for each paper, depending on the number of presentations in each session.
  • Ensure that the allocated time is not exceeded.
  • Introduce each presenter and facilitate the Q&A session.
  • Keep track of time and signal presenters when their time is running out.

Time Management Tips

  • For 30-minute presentations: 20 minutes for presentation + 10 minutes for Q&A
  • Provide a 5-minute warning before the end of each presentation
  • Be prepared to adjust timing if a presenter is absent
Additional Tips
  • Prepare a brief introduction for each paper
  • Have questions ready in case the audience is hesitant to ask
  • Collect presenter contact information for emergency situations
